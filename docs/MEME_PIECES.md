# 10th Grade Health team pieces

Teams choose one of eight original illustrated pieces during setup. Their chosen picture follows them through the first-team picker, board movement, score panel, finish order and winner celebration. Names remain visible in the selector, and the selection controls announce the piece and team to screen readers. All choices have identical rules, movement and scoring.

The characters are original vector artwork designed to read at small board-piece sizes. They use bold outlines and expressive faces alongside the game's forest green and gold theme. Assets are self-contained, served by Game Studio and require no social-media embeds, external image services or model calls during play.

## Compatibility

The eight existing saved avatar IDs (`fox`, `panda`, `frog`, `owl`, `dragon`, `penguin`, `robot`, `unicorn`) remain unchanged. Labels and image paths belong to the fixed UI catalog; they are not new fields in a saved team or game. Existing questions, teams, positions, points, dice and finish order retain the same version 1 validation and storage key. Returning to an older frontend can show the former animal artwork for these IDs without invalidating the saved match.

The school logo, question import format, published game formats, online party avatars, other games and host identity boundaries remain independent of this cosmetic update.
