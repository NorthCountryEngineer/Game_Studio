# Gracie question bank

The prepared bank contains 19 questions: 13 unique topics recovered from the supplied recording and the six selected additions. The duplicate removed during the recording is omitted. Spelling and grammar are normalized, and misleading or ambiguous claims are replaced with statements supported by the original information sources below.

Every card includes its correct answer, an explanation, an HTTPS source link and an APA-style plain-text reference. Sources were checked November 5, 2026. The prepared data is bundled with the fixed engine; loading, drawing, answering and citing cards make no model calls. The source opens only when a player clicks its link.

## Content review

- The unsupported 78.5% county figure is replaced with the report's qualitative majority finding. No exact replacement percentage is calculated from an unknown item denominator.
- The unsupported 62% / 30-day quitting claim is replaced with CDC's national estimate about cannabis use disorder, explicitly separated from county rates.
- The 6% / 30% caffeine poll is not described as a rehabilitation success rate. The card instead covers caffeine withdrawal using National Library of Medicine information.
- The social-gathering card names coffee and tea so the answer is specific. THC-related wording clarifies the perception, learning and coordination cards.
- A commercial treatment-price estimate is replaced by a question about how insurance and services affect out-of-pocket costs. It does not quote a county treatment price.
- The approximate 650 figure is retained as a chart reading for respondents to the 2024 county assessment. It is not an exact count or a statement about every county student.
- The six selected additions retain their question wording, answer choices and answer keys.

## Loading and preservation

**Use Gracie's Questions** appends only missing exact copies. It preserves existing cards, IDs, team settings and progress. A manual draft or insufficient bank capacity prevents a partial change. Repeated clicks add no duplicates.

To play only the reviewed bank, choose **New game** and then **Use Gracie's Questions**. The normal new-game confirmation keeps a previous local copy when browser storage is available. Existing older cards are not silently rewritten or removed.

The same bank is available as [TXT](../public/templates/gracies-questions.txt) and [JSON](../public/templates/gracies-questions.json). Upload only one format. Question-file sources are optional; older files remain valid. Manual entry also supports a source URL, APA citation and explanation.

The existing version 1 game save is unchanged. Sources use a separate bounded companion key matched to the exact game ID and full question content, ordered choices and correct answer. The previous frontend reads the game and leaves companion data intact. Unsourced older or custom cards reveal their correct answer and honestly state that no source was supplied.

Prepared questions and their answer keys are public application data, not confidential assessments. Raw recording files, screen images, OCR captures and runtime identifiers are excluded from the repository and frontend archives.
